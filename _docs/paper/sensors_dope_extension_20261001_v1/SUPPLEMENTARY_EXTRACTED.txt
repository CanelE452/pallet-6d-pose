1    Status and reading guide
Tables and figures copied from the original manuscript describe historical YOLO experiments. The DOPE and
ResNet18 extensions are identified separately below; no value is filled from another estimator. This supplement
preserves the distinction between completed reused-development evidence and independent confirmation. All three
matched-budget fits were completed; the learning curves are diagnostic, not a best-checkpoint selector. Independent
real-session confirmation is not yet available.
    The completed ResNet18 FULL estimator uses RGB and five registered-dimension values jointly. Historical
YOLO/DOPE heads remain image-feature based and use dimensions only in PnP. ResNet P0 is the primary third-
backbone transfer arm; P5/P5_CONSTANT separately tests a dimension input to the correction head. These roles
must not be merged into a single dimension-benefit claim.

accuracy tables still use the original frozen predictions.
    After all balanced timing samples passed, the model-release guard prevented reuse of the same process for
isolated memory measurements. The saved latency samples were retained unchanged; allocation was measured
in a new process for each model. A further untimed coordinate replay audit belongs to that memory phase, not
to the earlier timing samples. Per-sample timed numerical bounds passed, but individual drift magnitudes and
OpenCV/inter-operation thread observations were not saved before the guard stopped the process. Those missing
observations are explicitly distinguished from the separately recorded memory-phase settings. Recovery records and
both original attempts are retained; no fastest session is chosen.

8                   Research history and interpretation
Prior Hough/line, active-adaptation, replay and robustness investigations motivated subsequent decisions, but do
not become additional unseen validation sets. Old code, checkpoints, metrics and verdicts remain unchanged. This
selection history means DEV is reused development evidence. The fixed model is not redesigned in response to an
unfavorable prior comparison.
    The baseline checkpoint is not purely synthetic-from-scratch: the recorded initialization checkpoint identifies
COCO-pose training, despite a historical output directory containing the word scratch. P’s additional training uses
synthetic labels only. ImageNet supervision for the added prior is separately disclosed. These distinctions prevent
an overbroad claim that no real annotation contributed anywhere.

9    Independent data and annotation handoff
The actual collection and schema are in HUMAN_INPUTS_REQUIRED.txt and CONFIRMATION_SCHEMA.json. The
starting plan is12 separate sessions with15 prespecified frames each; at least30 frames receive two independent
prediction-blinded annotations and blind adjudication. The count is not a power guarantee. Preserve original
videos, timestamps, capture groups, camera/mount, calibration, actual object dimensions/identity and prior-use
declarations. Changed image encoding or filename does not prove independence from an old scene.
    Unreviewed provenance and an unfrozen comparator panel block confirmation only, not the completed develop-
ment experiment. New-frame reference geometry must be bound to its camera/dimensions/index convention before
a 6D claim. Human annotation, author identities, funding and consent cannot be fabricated by the CLI. These
obligations are separate from compilation and code checks.


10    Three-estimator scope and outstanding evidence
The third application is a completed full-image, nine-landmark Simple-Baselines-derived ResNet18. Its frozen
FULL estimator receives RGB and registered physical dimensions, and its primary transfer test adds the same
image-feature local refiner without a second dimension input (P0). D0 is the matched direct-residual control. P5
and P5_CONSTANT form a separate dimension-input extension. All real-image outcomes are reused-development
evidence. The completed accuracy and latency receipts are described below. Three measured frozen interfaces
provide finite application evidence, not a universal model-agnostic theorem or an independent test.

11     DOPE extension: frozen interface and training lineage
For frozen DOPE on reused DEV, the conditional keypoint median is 12.585 pixels before refinement and 7.461
pixels for the mean of three P seed statistics; full-GT PCK10 is 19.872 and 36.775 percent, respectively. The tables
retain missing predictions and unfavorable outcomes. The frozen checkpoint is weights/backbone_dope_final_
v1/run/final_net_epoch_0060.pth; its SHA-256 begins 0de80490cb3b4f9b, with the full digest in the protocol.
The actual original training loop includes epochs 0 through 60. Original DOPE training uses random crop 400
and its recorded rotation, color, noise, and Torch FP32 normalization. The fixed inference adapter instead follows
the existing reflect100–resizeback–short400 path with its existing NumPy-to-FP32 normalization. A historical
header value of 448 does not describe the executed crop. No equal-baseline-epoch or identical-training/inference-
normalization claim is made.
    The new adapter takes BGR input and returns explicit composed aﬀine matrices, native/canvas/network sizes,
semantic point validity, hull and confidence. Taps are post-ReLU 17/26; layer 16 alone is a convolution, not
the feature used. Anisotropic inverse restoration adds the transformed displacement to the retained FP64 original
point. This keeps a zero move exactly equal to the baseline and prevents a fresh FP32 reprojection from introducing
approximately 10−5 -pixel roundoff. Fixed image-size, corner-index, GT-padding, camera and dimension contracts
are required before scoring; there is no silent ID or geometry fallback.
    The added P and D heads share 256/stride4 and 128/stride 8 evidence, 16-channel projections and 24 encoded
channels. The backbone is in evaluation mode with gradients disabled. Newly initialized heads alone are optimized;
an existing YOLO head is not a warm start. The source protocol fixes three seeds, 6,000 updates per head, batch
16 and six final fits. The AdamW learning rate is 10−3 , weight decay 10−4 , betas (0.9, 0.999), warmup 100, cosine
terminal fraction 0.1, and gradient clip 5. These are head budgets, not convergence certificates for either family or
equality of baseline-training cost.
    Source prediction metadata is cached over the fixed source manifest; large full-resolution feature maps are not
retained as a training cache. Online frozen VGG feature extraction therefore contributes actual training cost.
Exact source image identities, usable rows, per-seed order hashes, final checkpoints, calibration temperatures and
selected movement rules belong to the new receipts. The source mask can differ from the historical 55,915 usable
YOLO rows. Nominal exposures must not be confused with successfully supervised corners; supported-frame loss
reductions are retained.

12     DOPE results, missingness and paired uncertainty
The completed DOPE fits use 44063 usable training rows, 22034 learned parameters for P and 22522 for D. Six final
fits each complete6000 updates with96000 nominal source exposures; no real images train these heads. P selects
λ = 1.0000 and original-image-diagonal cap none. D selects λ = 1.0000 and original-image-diagonal cap none. P
temperatures for seeds1–3 are 1.00, 1.00, 1.00. The seven fixed arms are baseline DOPE and D1–D3/P1–P3. The
complete population contains 319 positive images, 13 sessions and 2818 supervised keypoints. Every arm retains
every population row, including failed or incomplete predictions. The conditional nine-point statistic requires the
original box-IoU match and a finite nine-point prediction. Full-GT PCK counts excluded predictions as failures;
separately labeled partial-point PCK is diagnostic only. No additional 2689-negative DOPE AP result is implied.
    The evaluator reuses the canonical camera and dimension mapping, prediction-only choice of width or depth,
and SQPnP followed by LM refinement. It does not let the reference pose choose a prediction hypothesis. Fewer
than six finite corners, selector exceptions and failed PnP produce explicit failed rows. Pose medians use available
fits and are always accompanied by coverage with denominator 319. A changed support prevents a conditional
paired confidence interval rather than silently selecting a favorable intersection. Reference rotation/translation
remain geometry-derived, with no new physical metrology.
    Paired session and frame bootstrap reuse the existing seed 20260914 and 10000 draws. The family statistic
averages per-seed pooled statistics, not predictions, and compares with one baseline. Source-selected wrappers do
not use real outcomes, but the decision to run this second-estimator study followed earlier research on the same
DEV population. The intervals are therefore exploratory and unadjusted. No independent TEST exists in this
extension.

14     ResNet18 direct estimator and dimension inputs
The ResNet18 FULL estimator and all twelve D0/P0/P5/P5_CONSTANT head fits completed. P0 is the primary
third-backbone transfer arm; it improves central 2D development error and PCK10, while its translation interval
includes zero. P5 is reported only as a separate dimension-conditioned-head extension because P5–P5_CONSTANT
does not establish stable joint 2D/T/R improvement. The third estimator is explicitly a Simple-Baselines-derived
ResNet18 full-image pallet9 adaptation, not PVNet, PoseFix or a renamed DOPE stage. The ImageNet file is
resnet18-f37072fd.pth; the protocol records its full SHA-256. The 384 × 512 FP32 input uses aspect fitting
and centered letterboxing without a predicted or ground-truth crop. Three 256-channel deconvolutions produce
nine stride-four spatial distributions. Training minimizes 0.1 times normalized Gaussian spatial cross-entropy plus
smooth-L1 error of the spatial expectation. The earlier 60-epoch masked-MSE pilot failed its fixed usability check
and supplies none of the reported accuracy values.
    The dimension vector is computed in fixed object axes and normalized from synthetic TRAIN only. FULL
receives all five values through decoder FiLM, SHAPE receives the two ratios, and CONSTANT receives zeros
through the same architecture. Each arm uses the same seed-42 initialization, source order, augmentation and fixed
10-epoch budget. The completed direct-estimator study trains CONSTANT, SHAPE and FULL arms for a fixed
10 epochs on 55,980 synthetic TRAIN images (34,990 updates and 559,800 exposures per arm). All share seed 42,
ImageNet ResNet18 initialization, identical√ source order and photometric augmentation. FULL supplies normalized
log W , log D, log H, log(W/D) and log(H/ W D) to a decoder FiLM block; SHAPE keeps only the two ratios and
CONSTANT supplies zeros. Epoch 10 is fixed using no real-image selection.
    The direct FULL–CONSTANT comparison is mixed: DEV median changes by −0.210 pixels, reconstructed-
reference translation by +1.269 cm, and rotation by −0.760◦ . Plastic median worsens by 0.145 pixels. Wood
improves by 1.196 pixels but its depth 0.59 m lies below the synthetic TRAIN minimum 0.818 m. GREEN150 and
0918 improve in aggregate, but each square set uses one constant dimension vector; the 0918 paired frame count
is 59 improved versus 60 harmed with median change +0.031 pixels. These observations do not identify a stable
causal dimension benefit.

15     ResNet18 primary frozen-refiner transfer
FULL is frozen before head fitting. D0 and P0 use layer2/layer3 features at strides 8/16, the frozen predicted points
and hull, and the same source-selection principles as the other estimator applications. Each head has three seeds,
6,000 updates and batch 16. P0, with 22,034 trainable parameters, is the primary third-backbone transfer arm. D0
is the 22,522-parameter matched direct residual control. Neither receives a new dimension vector; FULL’s earlier
dimension conditioning is common to all rows.
    The frozen FULL baseline yields 55,804 usable synthetic TRAIN rows. D0, P0, P5 and P5_CONSTANT each
use three seeds of 6,000 AdamW updates at batch 16 (96,000 nominal exposures per fit) and zero real training
images. Trainable counts are 22,522 for D0, 22,034 for P0, and 23,331 for each P5 arm. Synthetic selection fixes
λ = 1 for every arm, a 0.01 image-diagonal cap only for D0, no cap for P0/P5, and temperature 1 for all probability
heads.
    P0 reduces conditional median and raises complete-denominator PCK10. Its session interval excludes zero for
2D median and canonical camera-facing-frame C2 rotation, while the translation interval includes zero. P90 changes
from 52.054 to 52.086 pixels, so no tail-dominance claim is made. All arms retain 299 matched frames and 319/319
PnP coverage because the forced single-pallet FULL hull and output support are fixed.

16     Separate ResNet18 correction-head dimension extension
P5 adds a zero-initialized candidate-logit residual from the normalized five-value vector. P5_CONSTANT has the
same module and initialization but supplies zeros. This is the incremental dimension-input contrast for the head.

in every arm.


17     ResNet18 integrated runtime and evidence limits
The integrated run measures FULL and one fixed seed for each head on the same image bytes used for YOLO and
DOPE timing. FULL+P0 adds 2.436 ms to paired full-path median latency; P5 adds 3.407 ms. The desktop result
includes model-specific preprocessing and all pose failures, but not file decoding, loading, Jetson export, concurrent
throughput or energy.
    All ResNet real-image panels are reused development data. The T/R reference is reconstructed from 2D an-
notation, camera intrinsics and registered dimensions rather than an independently instrumented 6D pose. Wood
is dimension-support extrapolation and square-set dimensions do not vary. These limitations remain even though
training, selection, evaluation and timing are complete.
