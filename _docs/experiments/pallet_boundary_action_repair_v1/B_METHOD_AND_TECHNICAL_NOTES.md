# B finite-support repair and separate N0-local package

This document describes registered algorithms. Accuracy is reported only after all predictions are sealed and independently scored.

B_FINITE_BANK reselects the exact original carried B_FIXED_BANK candidates. B_FINITE_FULL and EDGE_FINITE_FULL use the original four starts per W/D family, including the same prediction-only yaw proposals, and the original region/edge costs. B_FINITE_LOCAL starts twice from the identical physical N0 pose, once in each registered W/D representation, with no new yaw proposal. Its fixed-family conditional solve never selects an owner or W/D family internally. These are four separate experiment arms; the local/full difference includes both starts and regularization.

For a fixed query center c and normal n, candidate projected edge a→b has v=b−a and w=a−c. The actual infinite-line intersection has D=cross(n,v), offset d=cross(w,v)/(D·pixel_scale), and segment coefficient λ=cross(w,n)/D. A supported candidate requires positive original native-vertex depths, finite projection, nondegenerate edge, nonparallel normal and 0≤λ≤1 within fixed numerical tolerance. Off-canvas intersection is recorded; being off canvas alone does not invalidate a finite intersection. Original visibility, canvas and O/T/U constraints remain separately active.

The float64 tolerances are λ epsilon=8·sqrt(machine epsilon), minimum projected length=sqrt(machine epsilon)·max(1, original image diagonal), and parallel angle epsilon=sqrt(machine epsilon). The required denominator exceeds max(length epsilon, angle epsilon·edge length). They were derived from representation precision, before new accuracy scoring. No image-specific accuracy threshold was fitted.

Every supported profile from the immutable original bundle is always evaluated. The original likelihood, RGB, profile grids, reliability, group means and B replacement of complete/partial/outline evidence are unchanged. Unsupported candidate geometry retains its old diagnostic energy but is ineligible. It never gets zero energy, nearest-endpoint clipping, candidate-dependent observation deletion or a new none mixture. Duplicate serialization of the same fixed support adds no independent information.

During optimization a fixed denominator sign defines the seed's nonparallel connected component. Signed D, signed(cross(w,n)+epsilon·D), and signed((1+epsilon)·D−cross(w,n)) give analytic division-free support inequalities. The same final finite predicate is checked again. Analytic projection/constraint Jacobians propagate to the six pose parameters. Invalid/singular final geometry is rejected even if SLSQP reports success.

Feasible restoration uses damped linearized constraint correction and finite merit backtracking, at most 20 steps inside the original 80-iteration solve budget; SLSQP receives the remaining iterations. It creates no additional start or optimizer invocation. Restoration steps, trial calls, failure, and cumulative physical motion are recorded. Numerical damping is sqrt(machine epsilon)·max(1, ||A||²); a correction is capped to one dimensionless parameter unit. This is numerical restoration, not new image evidence or a guaranteed globally feasible initialization.

The local cost adds λR·||Log(R0ᵀR)||² + λt·||(center−center0)/registered_diagonal||². Rotation uses radians and translation uses the fixed registered extent diagonal. Its one train-only rule takes the median, across all original train768 frames, of the mean diagonal of automatic-observation 2JᵀJ in each rotation/normalized-translation block, with unit precision as a zero-information lower bound. Complete and partial rows use their original reliability, covariance and radial robust weight after B replacement. B uses its full discrete-profile Fisher information, group reliability and fixed-query mean. No reference, validation or real statistic is fitted. The resulting frozen precisions are λR=7750.212386319994 and λt=104492.61843444691; no strength search was performed. This single-frame cuboid transfer uses the Tikhonov principle described by SRT3D; it is not a reproduction of its tracker or numerical λ values.

Calibration counted train768, information768, complete2102 rows, partial0 rows (the historical D0 synthetic pipeline does not create those partial observations), B18366 profiles, fixed unsupported5371, invalid-at-N0 zero, no-information zero. It did not manufacture missing partial observations. This calibration does not make D0 a matched synthetic pipeline.

The candidate ledger stores optimizer_success, numeric_pass, original/final feasibility, finite_support, constraint violation, raw gradient norm, restoration and optimizer iterations, original fixed query counts, fallback source, and accumulated rotation/center/projection changes. Raw gradient norm is not a constrained KKT certificate. A finite feasible endpoint with optimizer_success=False can be an eligible numerical candidate, but is never reported as a converged correction. N0 fallback is separate from a supported new B output.

Ten expected-condition test categories are specified in test_finite_boundary.py before execution, covering finite versus infinite support, endpoint/singular/depth/canvas states, collapse, infeasible input, immutable universe, absent-input retention, analytic/one-sided/regularizer derivatives, scale/Q/duplicate/archive invariance, distinct statuses and independent linear-system intersections. The original three extreme outputs are fixed-pose geometry regressions, with all four AB owner mappings checked. They do not constitute new generalization measurements. Actual material owner and independent physical pose ground truth remain UNVERIFIED.

Sources actually inspected: [SRT3D §3.4/Eq.(30), §5.4](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-022-01579-8) regularizes incremental rotation/translation updates and discusses local initialization, geometry, color-separation and occlusion limits. Our cumulative N0-centered prior and train-only observed-information rule are a separate transfer design; SRT3D does not establish this rule's pallet accuracy. [SciPy 1.12 SLSQP documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.12.0/reference/optimize.minimize-slsqp.html) defines maxiter and ftol. We supply analytic Jacobians; its finite-difference eps setting is not our finite-segment tolerance. All model validity and support checks remain separate from optimizer termination.
