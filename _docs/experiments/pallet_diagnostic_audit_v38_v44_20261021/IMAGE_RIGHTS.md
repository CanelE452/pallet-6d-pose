# Image rights and interpretation

Only the six selected real pallet comparison panels are included. The user stated they personally captured these real photos and explicitly approved public publication of v38–v44 representative comparison figures. No full original dataset is included. The selected panels were visually checked for identifiable people, plates and document/contact text; PNG metadata is stripped. No generative image editing or pose adjustment was performed. Recording text in the annotation header was removed; the raw photo geometry and saved outputs are preserved.

Synthetic TRAIN/VAL photos, renderer backgrounds and textures are excluded because their external asset rights were not established. Scalar diagnostics are included without raw image/mesh/face-ID data. The numerical reference is reconstructed geometry, not independently measured physical GT. An apparently close silhouette does not certify W/D identity or 6D correctness. Auxiliary visual observations are not independent human annotations.

Figures cover a CAL selection injury, CAL all-method failure, OCPR success control, PRIMARY candidate absence, PRIMARY PF+SubPix success and PRIMARY N0 injury. They are diagnostic examples, not a representative performance sample. PRIMARY is repeatedly exposed development data.
